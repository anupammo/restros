The problem

Most restaurants run on four systems that disagree.

The POS has one menu. The aggregator dashboard has another. The kitchen works off a printed sheet with pen marks on it. By the time the owner reconciles them it is next month.

RestrOS collapses that into one catalogue with one source of truth. Change a price once and it lands on the counter, the pass, the QR menu and Swiggy in the same second.

Straight from a real menu card
11 items had no price printed on the card at all
4 prices existed only as pen marks in the margin
1 dish was sold every day but never made it onto the card

RestrOS reads the card, keeps the blanks as a visible “needs price” state, and turns the margin scribbles into suggestions the owner confirms with one tap.
